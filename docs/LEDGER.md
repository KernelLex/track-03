## Row schema

See `agent/ledger/models.py::LedgerEntry`. `seq`, `ts`, `prev_hash` and `hash` are assigned by `Ledger.append()` only — never caller-supplied (§15).

## Replay contract

`Ledger.replay(debtor_id, until=None)` folds one debtor's slice of the chain into a `ReplayResult` (`agent/ledger/store.py`). Verified by `tests/agent/test_shuffled_replay.py` (full pipeline, three shuffles, identical totals) and `tests/agent/test_ledger.py`.
