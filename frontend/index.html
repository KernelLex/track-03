📞 Voice call one-way

Places a real text-to-speech call. One-way by design in this build — there's no inbound capture for a spoken reply, unlike the two text channels.

To get all three on your own devices: put your number in the first field, then open Telegram, message @TrueCommit_bot (hi is enough) — it replies with your chat id. Paste that in the second field. Now every button, including Run the whole thing, reaches you, and replies you send on either text channel are answered.

Why Telegram needs an id rather than a number: a bot can only message someone who has messaged it first. That's a Telegram platform rule, not a limitation here — and it's also what stops this field being a way to message strangers, since an id you don't own simply won't accept anything from this bot. WhatsApp and calls have no such restriction. One caveat worth knowing: WhatsApp will accept a message for a number that has no WhatsApp account, and it will never arrive — so the status below shows Twilio's real delivery state, not just that the send was accepted.
